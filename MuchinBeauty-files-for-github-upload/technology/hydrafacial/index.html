What it is

Hydrafacial is a patented facial that uses gentle vortex technology to cleanse, exfoliate, extract and saturate the skin with nourishing serums — all in one relaxing session.

It is one of the most-requested facials in the world because it suits virtually every skin type, has no downtime, and leaves skin visibly brighter the same day.

How it works

  • Lymphatic drainageGentle suction supports detoxification and reduces puffiness for a more lifted look.
  • Cleanse + microdermabrasionA resurfacing tip lifts away dead skin cells to reveal smoother, brighter skin.
  • PeelA gentle acid blend loosens debris deep in the pores without irritation.
  • ExtractThe vortex tip painlessly clears blackheads and congestion from the pores.
  • Boosters (target)Targeted serums — for brightening, plumping or clarifying — are infused for your specific concern.
  • LED lightLED helps calm and support the skin to finish the treatment.
  • HydrateSkin is saturated with antioxidants and hydration to lock in the glow.

What to expect

Session: roughly 30–45 minutes, completely comfortable — most people find it relaxing.

Downtime: none. You can wear makeup and return to your day immediately.

Results: visibly brighter, smoother, more hydrated skin the same day. A monthly treatment keeps skin at its best.

Great before events — many clients book a Hydrafacial a day or two before a wedding, shoot or special occasion.

Frequently asked

Is there any downtime?
No. Hydrafacial is non-invasive with no downtime — you can apply makeup and resume normal activities right away.
How often should I get one?
Monthly is ideal for maintaining clear, hydrated, glowing skin, though even a single treatment gives an immediate glow.
Is it suitable for sensitive skin?
Yes — it is gentle and customisable, which is why it suits most skin types. We tailor the boosters to your skin at your appointment.
Does the extraction hurt?
No. Extractions are done with painless vortex suction rather than manual squeezing.
