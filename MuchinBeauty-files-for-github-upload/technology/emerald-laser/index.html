What it is

Emerald Laser is a low-level (cold) laser body treatment made by Erchonia®, a world leader in laser therapy. It is the only device FDA-cleared for full-body fat loss in people up to a 40 BMI — and it does it without surgery, needles, heat or recovery time.

Unlike treatments that freeze or burn fat, Emerald uses ten true green laser beams to gently coax fat cells to release their contents. The result is comfortable 30-minute sessions you can walk straight out of.

How it works

  • Ten 532nm green laser beamsEmerald delivers 10 true green (532nm) low-level laser beams across the treatment area — monochromatic, painless and non-thermal.
  • Fat cells release their contentsThe light creates temporary pores in fat cells so the fatty liquid inside can seep out. The cells shrink rather than being destroyed.
  • Your body flushes it naturallyThe released contents are carried away through your lymphatic system, the same way your body clears waste every day.
  • Fat cells behave like healthy cellsEmptied cells start functioning like lean, healthy cells — a key difference from fat-freezing, which destroys cells.

Why cold laser, not heat or freezing

Emerald is a non-thermal laser — there is no burning and no cooling, which is why sessions are painless with no reported side effects. The FDA labels its circumference results as non-temporary.

In Erchonia’s FDA clearance data, Emerald and its predecessors averaged roughly 6 inches (15.24 cm) of circumference loss across the waist, hips and abdomen over a typical course — among the strongest clinical evidence in the body-contouring category.

What to expect

Session: about 30 minutes, fully automated, lying down comfortably. Most people read or relax.

Downtime: none — it is a true walk-in, walk-out treatment with no pain or recovery.

Course: most clients do 6 or more sessions, one to three times per week. Pairing the course with simple healthy-lifestyle habits helps results last.

Areas: waist, hips, thighs and abdomen are most common, but Emerald can target stubborn fat almost anywhere, including arms.

Frequently asked

Does it hurt?
No. Emerald is a cold, non-thermal laser — there is no heat or cooling sensation and no recorded pain or downtime.
How many sessions will I need?
Most people start with 6 or more sessions, one to three times a week. Your plan is tailored at consultation.
Are the results permanent?
The FDA labels the results non-temporary. As long as you keep a normal-to-healthy lifestyle, results continue; significant overeating can add inches back.
Who is not a candidate?
Emerald is not recommended during pregnancy, with a pacemaker, with photosensitive epilepsy, or over an area of known cancer. We confirm suitability at consultation.
