What it is

Venus Epileve is a diode laser hair-removal device from Venus Concept that delivers gold-standard efficacy with a real-time cooling system for comfort.

Its 800nm wavelength and active cooling strike the balance between strong melanin absorption and deep penetration — making it safe and effective for all skin types, including tanned skin.

How it works

  • 800nm diode targets the follicleThe laser’s energy is absorbed by the pigment (melanin) in the hair follicle, disabling its ability to regrow hair.
  • Real-time cooling for comfortThe applicator’s temperature is monitored 1,000 times per second, delivering consistent cooling so treatment stays comfortable and safe on every skin tone.
  • PULSE mode — fewer sessionsHigher energy at shorter pulses for stronger results in fewer visits.
  • SLIDE mode — extra comfortContinuous motion with rapid low-intensity pulses and superior cooling for a quick, comfortable treatment.

Permanent reduction — and more

Venus Epileve is cleared for permanent hair reduction — a long-term, stable reduction in the number of hairs that regrow. It is also cleared for pseudofolliculitis barbae (ingrown hairs / razor bumps) and hirsutism.

Because the cooling system makes it safe across the full range of skin tones, it is a strong choice for clients who have been told they are ‘not a candidate’ for older lasers.

What to expect

Session: quick — small areas take minutes; you may feel a mild warm snap with each pulse, eased by the cooling.

Downtime: none. Mild redness may appear briefly.

Course: hair grows in cycles, so a series (commonly 6–8 sessions, spaced a few weeks apart) gives the best, longest-lasting reduction.

Areas: face, underarms, legs, bikini, back and more.

Frequently asked

Does it work on my skin tone?
Yes — the real-time cooling system makes Venus Epileve safe and effective for all skin types, including tanned skin.
How many sessions will I need?
Because hair grows in cycles, most people need a series (commonly 6–8) spaced a few weeks apart for the best permanent reduction.
Does it hurt?
Most people feel only a mild warm snap; the continuous cooling keeps it comfortable, and SLIDE mode is especially gentle.
Can it help with ingrown hairs?
Yes. Venus Epileve is cleared to treat pseudofolliculitis barbae — ingrown hairs and razor bumps.
